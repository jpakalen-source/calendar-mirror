Calendar Mirror – personal tool

Calendar Mirror is a private tool used only by its owner (jpakalen@gmail.com). It runs locally on the owner's Mac and copies events from Apple Calendar into the owner's own Google Calendar.

Privacy policy

  • The app accesses only the owner's Google Calendar.
  • Data is processed locally on the owner's Mac and is not sent to any other party or third-party service.
  • No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
